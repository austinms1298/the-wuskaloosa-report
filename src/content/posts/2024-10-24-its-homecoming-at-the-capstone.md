<p>Feel that chill in the air? It’s not Alabama’s fan base throwing shade… it’s just cool enough now that Homecoming week has arrived at the Capstone!&nbsp;</p><p>And at the request of many, I’m running for Homecoming Queen…again!&nbsp;</p><p>Last year, I asked for Homecoming Queen platform suggestions, because what I thought were winners in the past did not resonate with these kids today. And I dug deep to address some critical issues that would make this world a better place! Some past platforms include…</p><p><strong><em>Cut out the Mullet! </em></strong>(A much needed campaign)</p><p><strong><em>Done with the man bun! </em></strong>(Apparently, attacking men’s hair styles does not hack it these days)</p><p><strong><em>Public thrashings for computer hackers! </em></strong>(Who knew so many people sympathized with computer hackers that I would not win this year?!)</p><p><strong><em>Slow cars stay in the right lane!</em></strong></p><p>(“<strong><em>Right Lane Slow! Left Lane GO!</em></strong>” as Michael Kirkpatrick perfected with this catchy slogan)</p><p>And I really thought Susan Haynes came up with the winner for me last year…a vow that if I won HC Queen I would<strong><em> Bring Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce to Homecoming! </em></strong>(Apparently, people were sick of them or I thought for sure the crown was mine in 2023)</p><p>But alas, still no crown for me. There were several incredible runner-up platforms that still give me hope! Such as:</p><p><strong><em>NIL for mascots.&nbsp;</em></strong></p><p>Great idea looking out for the true entertainment guys by Georgia Rhodes. If Alabama has the Million Dollar Band, Big Al should be making some bank. Nice work, Georgia!</p><p><strong><em>Forget about student loans till graduation, line up some shots and let’s get loose tonight.&nbsp;</em></strong></p><p>Shout out to Joe Giglotto for this great suggestion and proving you know how to do college better than any of us. Well done!</p><p><strong><em>Alabama goes to Natty regardless of any or how many losses!</em></strong></p><p>A brilliant platform and very impressed with Jimmy Bank’s lack of interest in facts and stats when determining who the national champs should be…Alabama, enough said. We hear you, Jimmy, and this platform is in my future.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p><strong><em>Bring back the Wile E. Coyote and Roadrunner Cartoons.&nbsp;</em></strong></p><p>John Wuska, a worthy cause, but I think this one is out of my pay grade. Appreciate the love, bro!</p><p>&nbsp;And this year’s winning platform from Tuscaloosa’s own Will Grimsley:</p><p>&nbsp;<strong><em>Bring back paper tickets</em></strong>!</p><p>Will is surprisingly under 40 and still feeling the pain of the mobile ticket era we over 40 patrons detest…may not get the hippy vote killing the trees and all, but I think this could be my year…so feeling it! Go out and vote for me, and your worries about transferring your tickets incorrectly are over!</p><p>Enjoy Week 8! RTR!&nbsp;</p><p>Jackie&nbsp;</p>
