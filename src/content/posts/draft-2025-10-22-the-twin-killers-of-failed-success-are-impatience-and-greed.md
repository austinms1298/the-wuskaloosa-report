And it would never have happened if the volatiles had not been greedy and impatient! They could have easily gone to the locker room with a field goal facing a much more manageable score of 16–10 in the second half. But they got a little too big for their britches resulting in a glorious 10-point swing in favor of the Tide. </p><p>What was josh heupel’s half time talk like in UA’s Visitors <em>Fail Locker Room</em>? “Sorry team. That’s on me. We will not risk an endzone interception like that again…except on the two-point conversion. Otherwise, we’ve got this… it’s only 23-7 and we have a whole half to more than double the points we got in the first half to tie this thing up! So, while we can’t really be patient on this one…time is a wastin’…let’s get out there and get 40 more points at least! It’s great to be a tennessee vol, holler, holler”…and other hillbilly expressions in true rocky top nature. </p><p>&nbsp;(Excuse me, I have to take a break to watch the video above again…please join me!) </p><p>&nbsp;Ok, back now. We are going into Week 9 of football season, and poor auburn has not even&nbsp;scored 20 points in their sec games?! But on the bright side – no one is going to accuse the aubs of being greedy. In fact, their offense has been downright altruistic to its opponents, and they have demonstrated extreme patience not pulling the trigger on coach freeze. Let’s hope their approach of indifference pays off, and they can go bowling again in Birmingham this year.&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;But we did see impatience take over down in gainesville as the gators fired ncaa coach #9 of the season.
