<p>If every football game Saturday is an “Upset Saturday” is it more accurate to call our Saturdays this football season “<em>Expect</em> the Upset Saturday?” As a result of all the upsets this football season, there are only three teams undefeated going into week 9?!</p><p>I totally understand why this past Saturday was an exceptionally upsetting day to so many <em>despite the Tide being victorious against the aggies</em> and truly appreciate all who share my pain and regret in my failed attempt at UA Homecoming Queen…again. I guess my platform of <em>NIL for mascots</em> will continue to go unheard – the students apparently think poor Big Al should continue to go penniless despite working inside of an elephant skin in late August/early September at football games?! Shame, shame, shame.&nbsp;</p><p>Outside of the mascot world, NIL is truly creating parity in the college football system. Alabama and many other power football programs used to be three deep in future NFL caliber players on our rosters. Now with NIL, #2, #3 and #4 on the roster can cruise on for more money and playing time if they become unhappy. So, when the starter gets injured it is game over for those who used to seamlessly reload from the bench…meaning each week we are seeing new schools in the Top 25 even the Top 10.&nbsp;</p><p>I do not know about you, but I am not ready for this equality experience in our football world<em>. Uniformity</em> (excluding football unis, of course),<em> fairness, and balance</em> have its place in society for sure, but in college football?! NO WAY! Speaking on behalf of Alabama’s former oppressive football regime…I’m going to have to say when it comes to NCAA college football, <em>these standardization words</em> are nothing but the pr of commies! There’s no need for Alabama fans to take our turn slumming at the Music City Bowl…that’s not justice that’s cruel behavior.&nbsp;</p><p>I’m not even sure what to do about it either…maybe if I had some authority because I won Homecoming Queen I could do something...but…alas...(heavy sigh).&nbsp;</p><p>Well, the upcoming bye week gives us time to grieve my loss of the crown (again), regroup, and celebrate the GOAT’s birthday this Saturday with a full day of stress-free football. It’s not all competitive football, but the boogs are going to face the fighting pete’s in oxford in the morning and the “world’s largest outdoor cocktail party” between kirby’s puppies and the gators takes place on the <em>neutral site</em> of Atlanta, Georgia…about as neutral as if Alabama or the aubs played georgia in Birmingham, right?&nbsp;The night games are not crazy spiritied either, but if you pour a drink for the “world’s largest outdoor cocktail party” (because it would be rude not to) you might fall asleep early or then again…who knows what strangeness we may witness on the field on <em>Halloween</em> Saturday!</p><p>The bottom line is use this time to relax and enjoy the bye week, give your lucky undies time to rest and heal, and RTR!</p><p>Jackie&nbsp;</p>
