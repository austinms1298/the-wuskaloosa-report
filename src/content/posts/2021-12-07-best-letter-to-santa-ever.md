<p>OH MY! Last Saturday may be the greatest sports day ever for Alabama fans…ever! It was kind of sad to wake up and realize we didn’t have any more bulldogs to play this week. </p><p>Hopefully, Santa is bringing more December goodies to his favorite team who also dresses in his signature Crimson and White. But if you don’t ask, you may not receive soooo…we better ask for what we want! </p><p>&nbsp;<em>Dear Santa, </em></p><p><em>&nbsp;In addition to the SEC Championship we won this year, Alabama would like:</em></p><ul><li><em>&nbsp;a big announcement December 11</em><sup><em>th</em></sup><em> of Bryce Young being awarded the Heisman, </em></li><li><em>a play-off game victory that crushes the spirit and soul of all teams outside the Power Five conferences who hope to be in a future play-off game AND since we have been really good this year,&nbsp;&nbsp;</em></li><li><em>we are going to need additional love from you that lasts into the new year that completes this trifecta ask with a National Championship win (by any number of points you deem appropriate – seriously, your call here) on January 10</em><sup><em>th</em></sup><em>. </em></li></ul><p><em>&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>Now, Santa, don’t say that is too much to ask or call us greedy, because…well, we got all this last year too. It was really fun though. So, no need to go to the trouble of shopping or having your elves think up something new to build for us – we will take the same ol’ same ol’ again if that’s cool with you. Santa, we just want to be considerate of <u>your</u> time and make things easier on <u>you</u>, because, yes, we really are that kind and gracious of a people.</em></p><p><em>&nbsp;Thank you for your consideration of our gift requests.</em></p><p><em>&nbsp;Love, Alabama Fans Everywhere</em></p><p><em>&nbsp;P.S. Let’s go ahead and get on your list for next year Will Anderson for the Heisman since he led the nation in sacks <u>and</u> tackles for loss <u>and</u> was robbed this year from even an invitation to New York. But big congrats to Will on the being named the Defensive Player of the Year with the Nagurski Award! Thank you!</em></p><p>Enjoy this bowl season and have a blessed holiday! I will miss you crazy, avid football fans (found my people) and college football so much. Also, R.I.P. Cecil Hurt. His tremendous talent will not be forgotten and will be missed by so many. </p><p>&nbsp;Until next season...be safe and well. Let’s show the world who is the boss of college football in our bowl games SEC and Roll Tide! </p><p><br></p><p>Jackie</p>
