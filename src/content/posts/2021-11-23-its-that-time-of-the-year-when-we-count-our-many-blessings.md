<p>&nbsp;But...it is rivalry weekend that brings out the best and worst in us…so, back by popular demand…it’s time to get your <em>hate-on</em> in between bouts of gratitude. You know how it’s done – you just need to fine tune your nice/nasty skills. Now don’t say, “Who me!? I don’t know how to be nice/nasty.” Yes, you do. We’ve all got it in us, unfortunately/fortunately. </p><p>For example, you might say to your LSU buddy, “Don’t be down about losing Coach O. It only cost you $25M+ to make a positive change. There are lots of Division 3 coaches you can afford who would love to have your job.”</p><p>To the Aggie in your life, “Jimbo’s speech is a little easier to understand than Coach O’s. You must feel really good about the great investment you made in his ten-year contract.”</p><p>When you see that Gator family member, “I know your season has not turned out as you would like, but at least you can be confident that your program is headed in the right direction.” (Careful not to choke on your pumpkin pie when you say this one.)</p><p>That Auburn cousin needs to be asked, “When does your deal with the devil to get all the good calls/luck run out? You’ve had a great success with it through the years but may need to check your contract…have not seen it working since the first half of your Mississippi State game.”</p><p>To your favorite Clemson fan you can throw some shade with, “Gosh, y’all have had such great teams the last few years. I hope you make it to a bowl this year. That would be really disappointing for you to miss a trip to Shreveport in December.”</p><p>To the dreaded Vols family member, “Please pass the mustard…wait, DON’T!…I’ll get it myself.”</p><p>You totally got this. Just throw in some accolades then mix in the messages of doubt. You are smart enough to pull this off…or are you? Think of it as pulling off the best show of gratitude of all...that you ain’t them!</p><p>RTR and Happy Thanksgiving – grateful for all of you! Beat the Aubs!</p><p>Jackie</p><p>&nbsp;</p>
