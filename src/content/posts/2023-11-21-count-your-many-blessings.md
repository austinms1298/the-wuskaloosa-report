You know how it’s done – you just need to fine tune your nice/nasty skills. Now don’t say, “Who me!? I don’t know how to do that.” Yes, you – we’ve all got it in us.&nbsp;</p><p>For example, you might say to your michigan buddy, “Your head coach is having a <em>record breaking</em> season…<em>least number of games allowed to coach in a season due to cheating</em>.
