Now don’t call me a commie, but I think I have the perfect solution for <u>evening</u> the playing fields during this year’s bowl season. It’s pretty obvious, but the only <em>truly </em>fair resolution is to require the teams who played six or less games to wear ankle weights for the first half of each of their upcoming games so their players can feel as worn out as the players who had much longer seasons. Wearing ankle weights the<em> entire</em> game would be over the top, right? So, they just need to wear them for the <em>first </em>half. And nothing crazy weight-wise...something like... five pounds… probably won’t even notice it. And, if we can all agree and implement this protocol quickly, the “underplayed” can start practicing tomorrow to get used to this minor uniform alteration.
