<p>There you have it - right there in this adage…the source of Alabama fans’ high expectations, assumptions we will always win and total dismay when the world dares to oppose our “winning” plan. These are the words of wisdom we have been raised on that were instilled with Coach Bryant’s national championship success and then reinforced with Coach Saban’s amazing championship tenure. It’s not our fault we have these expectations…it’s just how we were brought up <em>to do </em>college football. We can’t help it and wouldn’t want to change it if we could. If you do not understand it, then you are not one of us, and yes, we admit we have felt sorry for you many times…until now…now…that our team is no longer <em>delivering</em> but actually <em>receiving</em> joyless murder ball. And, all of a sudden, we realize that our outlook on college football may be a little irritating at times to others (ok, probably all the time). But don’t be haters.&nbsp;</p><p>Do you hate ducklings because upon hatching they can jump into a pond and immediately start swimming without going to the local Y for swim lessons? Of course not, that’s just adorable instincts like Alabama fans’ presumptious optimism, right? Do you despise calves, foals or fawns for standing up and running within minutes of being born without any instruction at all? That would be silly and if it were a video on social media you would watch it over and over, “like it” and repost it just like <em>2nd and 26 Tua to Smitty</em> <em>for the Natty…</em></p><p><a href="https://www.youtube.com/shorts/wB-6__jHC8g" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">https://www.youtube.com/shorts/wB-6__jHC8g</a></p><p>(Continuing to write with tears of nostalgia in eyes)</p><p>Or what about opossums who do not <em>choose</em> to pretend to be dead when threatened…but whose bodies automatically shut down into a catatonic state emitting a foul odor that resembles rotting meat…which also may have been Alabama’s coaching strategy for Tennessee this weekend? (Yeah, maybe this example is not as endearing…heavy sigh.)&nbsp;</p><p>Point is…there’s no need to loathe or resent Alabama fans. We are actually tremendously gracious winners, because we are so used to winning…it’s just how we roll, literally. That’s why we are generally delightful, and if you’re not an Alabama fan, you should embrace the positivity of your local Crimson Tide neighbor and hope it’s contagious.&nbsp;</p><p>However, after this second loss of our season, you may need to do a wellness check on us…be extra kind…and don’t gloat to our faces (knock yourself out behind our backs…we do the same to you), but we are in a little bit of crisis mode…it’s the third year in the DeBoer era of seeing growth at the front end of the season before regression begins…and it is taking a toll on us. Our loss two Saturdays ago to the fighting kirbys was not embarrassing in the least. However, our ut rivalry was so much more fun when Alabama won at home <em>and</em> on the road against the volatiles. Can I get an Amen, Alabama fans?&nbsp;</p><p>So, here we are approaching the back end of the season, and we are starting to see the trend of decline. It hurts. We all bought in for the first six games seeing the progress. But this week we did not convert a third down until the second half finishing just 2/13 overall in third down conversions. We also incurred 10 penalties and four turnovers and missed 23 tackles. There was never a sense our coaches saw any answers for what the vols were dishing out to us. And no, it doesn’t make us feel better that half the Top 10 lost this week. No Alabama fan is asking, “Who’s good this year?”</p><p>This debate has no appeal…the answer used to be “Alabama” hands down every day of the week.&nbsp;</p><p>How do we get excited about moving forward?
