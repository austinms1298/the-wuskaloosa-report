<p>You know, I was impressed with the job the SEC (football conference not securities and exchange commission) did in selecting each of our respective teams’ three permanent rivals. It seemed fair, and you could tell the process required much contemplation from various viewpoints. However, the true depth of the philosophic review of our love/hate relationships with opposing teams only became clear to me after the Alabama - georgia game.</p><p>In the end, Alabama’s three rivals made sense: auburn, tennessee and the addition of mississippi state due to geographic region. However, there was a lot of discussion amongst fans about other opponent options. For example, our now <em>ex-steady girl</em> lsu could have been on the table as an eternal schedule partner not only due to our home field advantage in both Tuscaloosa and <em>at</em> death valley over the years (56-27-5) but also Coach Saban’s ties to lsu making the tigers loath playing Alabama each year more than they dread their annual Fat Tuesday hangover that starts on Ash Wednesday and ends sometime around Palm Sunday…plenty of passionate feelings on both sides of this match up.&nbsp;</p><p>However, georgia also seemed to be a possibility for our #<em>3 on our schedule/last in our hearts</em> permanent rival. The fervor between our schools grew to new heights with hardware on the line for SEC championship rings and even a couple of national championships. I know a lot of us did not want to see the athens bulldogs named as Alabama‘s third rival. Though, after Saturday’s game, I’m not sure what our concern was since it is apparent to all we completely <em>own </em>georgia?!&nbsp;</p><p>Likely, by now everyone is aware Alabama is 9-1 since 2015 against the puppies. Overall, Alabama holds the series lead 45-26-4.&nbsp;</p><p>But more fun facts pointed out by Nick Kelly of Al.com:</p><p>Georgia won 29 consecutive games from 2021-2023…<em>Alabama ended it.</em></p><p>Georgia won 42 Regular season consecutive games from 2020-2024…<em>Alabama ended it.&nbsp;</em></p><p>Georgia won 33 Consecutive home games from&nbsp;&nbsp;2019-2025…<em>Alabama ended it.&nbsp;</em></p><p>That being the case, I guess the SEC got sentimental and decided Alabama needed to see if the kirbys really loved us and let them go free by NOT making them our permanent third rival. But when the pups do come back…maybe in the playoffs sometime or the regular season one more time next year, we will know and certainly <em>they</em> will know that they are and always will be <em>ours.&nbsp;</em></p><p>But as you may recall, we felt this same jubilation last year after our georgia victory only to face the same immediate adversary we have coming back into our lives this Saturday. A team who has gotten a little haughty after pounding us last year for the first time since 1984 (back when the #1 hit was <em>Prince and the Revolution</em>’s “Purple Rain”…yes, it was that long ago) and is now coming into Bryant Denny, for <em>Game Day</em> no less, thinking they can beat us in academics <em>and</em> athletics this year. Their qb even had the gall to say, “Tuscaloosa will be a good, fun time.” We are not planning any fun at all for you, small superman but appreciate your representing&nbsp;vandy scholars everywhere so well with your extensive vocabulary on the matter.&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;This toxic relationship needed to be dissolved – thank you SEC – no one should date a narcissist, and so, commodores we are going to return to the 1980’s to beat you at your own game using the words of the Commodores we love (Lionel Richey style) and putting you on notice that we are “Leaving you Tomorrow” and it’s going to be “Easy” because we are better than you “Still” and if all fails we will give the ball to Kayden Proctor who will run through you like a “Brick House” and maybe you defeated us once, but you are not going to beat us twice in a row or even “Three Times a Lady”… because we <em>own</em> you too, vandy (60-20-4)!&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>Enjoy Week 6 and let’s beat the commodores…not the band (Lionel is from Alabama for goodness sakes) but the team, of course! RTR!&nbsp;</p><p>Jackie&nbsp;</p>
