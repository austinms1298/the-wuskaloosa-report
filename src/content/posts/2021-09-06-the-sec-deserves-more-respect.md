<p>I know we should be grateful for a wonderful start to college football…Bryce Young came out looking like a seasoned veteran, Dabo lost a legit college football game (two in a row now to be exact) before Clemson resumes their high school football schedule and we even had fans cheering in-person in their respective stadiums! BUT what’s up with streaming SEC football games!? I am personally offended for you Auburn, South Carolina and Arkansas for not meriting a real television station to show your games this past weekend!</p><p>Where’s the respect? This is the SEC?! That means every football team (even you Vandy) deserves to be on regular television stations. Not to mention while ranting…this upcoming weekend…in addition to three streamed games, we have three games televised at 11 am, one at noon and two at 2:30 pm!? How are we going to see all our precious football with this type of crammed together scheduling? Better start icing your hands now to avoid finger cramps flipping the remote from game to game. Come on SEC Office, stand up for our football viewing rights! #FreetheSECnow!</p><p>Speaking of freedom on a more serious note – you may be aware Saturday is the 20<sup>th</sup> Anniversary of 9/11. Despite our issues and differences, we can all agree we live in the best country in the world. Let’s take a moment during the national anthem to remember our many blessings living in America. U.S.A.!!…And, of course, Roll Tide! </p><p>Enjoy Week 2 of college football,</p><p>Jackie</p><p><br></p>
