<p>This nose metaphor is brought to you by the NCAA College Football Playoff Committee. Alabama is <em>your nose</em>! And, <em>unsurprisingly</em>, georgia is your <em>friend’s </em>(friends in just this scenario) <em>nose</em> not getting picked! Woohoo! The Tide broke that winning streak…no threepeat for kirby…going to have to start over with your championship count like we did at Alabama, kirbs. Can I get an Amen?! From the first play of the SEC Championship game with Kendrick Law’s brilliant kickoff reception…catching the ball with one foot out of bounds so georgia started with a penalty to Will Reichard’s outstanding kicks winning the NCAA all-time scoring record, it just felt like a win was possible!&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;In the big picture of the CFP selections, I do feel for fsu being denied the opportunity to play for the National Championship but not enough to wish otherwise. Their frustration is understandable. They are undefeated, and it is not their fault their conference opponents have become the equivalent of high school teams. But the issue lies in that when you are in high school, you may be able to use your <em>fake id </em>100% of the time<em> </em>to buy beer from your buddy Bubba Joe at the Circle K down the street, but when the ABC board busts you in a bar with that <em>fake id</em>, they recognize it as an illegitimate identification every day of the week.&nbsp;</p><p>Focusing on the positive of the day and for all the cursing and scares, this Alabama team brought so much joy this season. They are absolutely my most fav team ever, and it’s not over yet!&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;It’s bowl and holiday season(s)! Since Alabama fans have chosen Santa’s colors of Crimson and White, we have the <em>VIP membership deal </em>on holiday sports gifts.&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;So, Santa, we deserve some more nice stuff to come our way. We behaved really well this year…our team has improved every single week and our GOAT head coach still has not called a single prostitute on his state-issued cell phone (hugh freeze remains a fun hire for the rest of us), and we Alabama fans have officially learned to be <em>grateful</em> fans. So, <em>please </em>Santa, this year under the tree we would like:</p><p><em>Designed by your very best elves</em>…</p><p>A bag of Jalen Milroe’s tenacity for each of us on those days when everyone seems to doubt us,</p><p>A National Championship ring so we can wear our rings on all our fingers and almost finish our toes,</p><p>A home video of kirby’s reaction during the NCAA CFP selection show,&nbsp;</p><p>For fsu AD michael alford, a bottle of red and a bottle of white WHINE,&nbsp;</p><p>Regular bottles of red and white wine for the CFP committee,&nbsp;</p><p>Switches and coal for jim harbough for being suspended as often as he coached during the regular season due to cheating,</p><p>An Alabama return punter in 2024 who is a combo of David Palmer and Javier Arenas…Javier Palmer or David Arenas…either one works and, finally… </p><p>A necessary evil, NIL money in our stocking!</p><p>Love, Your Alabama Kids&nbsp;</p><p><br></p><p>Hope you all enjoy the bowl season! Regardless of how it turns out, a big THANK YOU to the talented 18-21 year olds (unless you are players granted extra COVID year(s) and are now the age of 35) who give us such joy and pain during football season and to our coaches who are willing to make millions of dollars to keep us entertained or like jimbo…to just go away!&nbsp;</p><p>Much love to you all and wishes for a wonderful holiday season…and, of course, RTR!&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;Jackie&nbsp;</p>
