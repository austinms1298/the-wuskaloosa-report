<p>Sooooo…what was your first impression of the Tide this season?…Can we make it to the playoffs? Can we win a majority of our games and go to a bowl game? Can we win four games? Will Coach Saban pick us to beat our opponents on <em>Game Day</em>? (Oh, that’s going to sting if he does not. You know that man loves Alabama, but, heaven knows, the GOAT hates losing any contest of any kind.)</p><p>I guess perhaps some of the worst news for Alabama fans is that this is not really our first impression. The “first” impression was last year. This was the regroup, rebuild, reset year. But, instead, on Saturday, we experienced what it’s like to be an auburn fan during football season: we lost our game, our state rival won, and we know there is more of this to come this year…could be even worse than the two most repugnant words we have come to love for the aubs…<em>Birmingham Bowl</em>. Don’t speak it into existence!</p><p>To my fellow <em>statesmen</em> who receive this email of the tiger persuasion…I salute you. I had no idea the strength and fortitude it takes to face football season knowing you will not end up being the national champions or at least in the discussion until the very end of the season. You have so much to teach us about how to deal with our new circumstances. Life lessons we must face such as…it’s ok to plan meetings and events in January on the same nights as the football semifinals and championship games - chances are you will not care so much about the outcomes. Also, perhaps, do not watch so many sports shows expecting to hear how elite your team is…you can bet what is said will hurt your feelings some. In addition, doing your laundry promptly after game day each week to ensure you can wear your lucky undies to the next football game is now optional…not required uniform. (Well, that’s just going too far. We are not animals - no need to completely abandon all dignity and hope.) And, (heavy sigh) we must support our team and coaches, because they are OUR team and coaches. (This last one is seriously a tribute to AU where UA fans struggle with this duty.)</p><p>Well, boogs, we appreciate your example, and we will be reaching out to you for more input on how to adapt to our new phase of football life. Please be patient with us…we are not going to be at our best. This is going to be hard for us…remember, we are more used to having a National Championship AND a Heisman in the same year as opposed to losing our season opener - and that’s how we <em>prefer</em> to roll. But <em>Roll Tide</em> anyway!&nbsp;</p><p>Enjoy Week 2 and let’s beat LA Monroe!</p><p>Jackie&nbsp;</p>
