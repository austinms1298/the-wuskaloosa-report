<p>When will this miserable feeling of a 40-year winning streak ending&nbsp;&nbsp;to knock you out of first place go away? Could have been a perfect weekend with aubs and tennessee losing too. 😞Sure, the sun came up after Alabama’s loss to vandy, but we know it didn’t really want to… it was just doing its job.&nbsp;</p><p>And to be fair, lots of people did <em>not</em> do their jobs last Saturday…including us.&nbsp;</p><p>I did not remind one person to do laundry nor did I wear my lucky undies. “Why waste them on the commodores?” we&nbsp;&nbsp;said nonchalantly?! (I even used to capitalize “vandy” to be nice and show respect for their academic prowess. That’s done…but still have to love their qb.)</p><p>UA even had a basketball nil shindig Saturday before the game?! Inappropriate. It’s football season y’all.&nbsp;</p><p>We all took the game too lightly.&nbsp;</p><p>People being fashion police on our coach’s attire when the only real fashion faux pas was two #2’s on the field for Alabama.&nbsp;&nbsp;Players having bad attitudes and embarrassing us with their classless behavior. Being outcoached, outplayed and outdone by not just an unranked team but a <em>never</em> ranked team.&nbsp;</p><p>There’s a lot of blame to go around, but no one‘s mentioned the real culprit - the DCC. Who is that you say? The Designated Cussing Coach we discussed needing at the beginning of the season to replace Saban’s vigor! Who knows if that hire was even made or not?! But, again, I think we all know 20 or 30 people who would forfeit their retirement account, their kids’ college fund and most of their belongings for that job…even if it were on a volunteer basis. I say we find out who the DCC is and evaluate their experience and education.&nbsp;&nbsp;Are they really qualified for this responsibility? If he/she cannot get the Tide appropriately fired up, then we may need an EDCC (Executive Designated Cussing Coach) to supervise this sitch. I know, I know, now it’s starting to sound like a government project with all the hierarchy, but this is important stuff - maybe the Department of Defense could fund it…since <em>our</em> defense struggled so much Saturday?&nbsp;</p><p>Let’s learn from our pain as <em>His Airness</em> suggests…do our jobs of being cocky but delightfully fun Alabama fans and let our pain med be <em>crushing everybody else</em> this year! Do your laundry!&nbsp;</p><p>Enjoy Week 7 and RTR!</p><p>Jackie</p>
