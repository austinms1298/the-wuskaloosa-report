<p>So, y’all did your laundry and wore the lucky drawers! Nice work Alabama fans. After the LSU game, my cute mom suggested the reverse might be required – the game planners focus on clean laundry and the fans hone in on the game plan!</p><p>Well, since the first half of the ole miss game was as bad as it gets, and the second half was what we know to be ALABAMA FOOTBALL…laundry is still not our issue. It’s bigger than that and others are noticing. If you don’t believe me, the football television gods have assigned us the crummy 11 am kick-off this weekend against Austin Peay…home of every Kindergarten boy’s (or any boy of any age actually) favorite cheer “Let’s Go Peay!” Even worse, we do not even merit a <em>real</em> television station but are relegated to the streamed sec network+ channel. Show some respect for the GOAT, people. This may be an off year for Alabama with our <em>monstrous</em> two losses by a <em>combined total</em> of four points, but any other team would gladly change places with our record over the past decade. So, don’t get all full of yourselves all you <em>other</em> football teams or we will beat you in soccer just for spite.&nbsp;</p><p>Alabama peeps…keep the faith…it’s going to be ok. Most of us do not run to the pain meds the moment our head starts to bother us a little. The pain has to intensify, you have to tell <em>at least</em> three people your head hurts and they ask you, “Have you taken anything for it?” And then…finally…we find the bottle of Excedrin Migraine Formula and take two to ease the pain. Coach Saban took the Excedrin to ease the pain when he adapted and changed Alabama’s offensive scheme bringing Lane Kiffin to Tuscaloosa, and we ruled the land.&nbsp;Now, we simply require another Excedrin formula to address the excruciating pain created by offensive and defensive coordinators. In GOAT we trust!</p><p>On the bright side, Jimbo’s loss to the the aubs won him last place in the sec west. Way to go aubs - you showed tx am (aggies 3-7 now for the year) who was boss and celebrated the victory in the stands after the game longer than the total time of the aggies’ ball possession. Coach Carnell is looking good on the plains.
