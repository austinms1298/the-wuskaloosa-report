Alabama Opens the Season With a Statement

A dominant first half, a secondary that flew around, and an offense that looked exactly like what we hoped it would be in August.

There's a version of this opener where we spend all week talking about what went wrong. This isn't that. Saturday night at Bryant-Denny was as clean a season debut as you're going to see from a program that had real questions coming in — and those questions got answered loud and fast.

The Defense Was The Story

We came into the year wondering whether the linebackers could cover. We came in wondering whether the corners were ready. Through three-and-a-half quarters, the answer was a resounding yes to both. The pressure packages worked. The zone coverage disguised things well enough that the opposing quarterback never found a rhythm.

This is a real defense. Not a soft schedule, feel-good defense. The kind that goes on the road in October and wins games you have no business winning on offense alone.

A Few Things To Watch Going Forward

The run game was serviceable but not dominant — something to keep an eye on before the SEC schedule kicks in. And the kicking situation still feels unsettled. But those are quibbles. This is a team that looks like it knows what it is. Roll Tide.
